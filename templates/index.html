What Trackify does

A budget tracker that shows its work.

Log an expense or a paycheck in one line, tag it by category, and the dashboard updates right away. No spreadsheets, no monthly reconciliation, no guessing where the money went.

Log it in seconds

Type a plain-language line like "spent 12 on lunch," or use a quick form. Categories and dates fill themselves in.

See the pattern

Dashboard charts break spending down by category and by week, updated the moment you log something new.

Ask a straight question

The built-in advisor answers plain questions about your budget with plain answers, no jargon.

Stay on top weekly

An optional weekly recap lands in your inbox summarizing what changed since last time.
